About Our Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is committed to providing fresh, high-quality coffee beans to our customers. We are a reliable and well-organized plantation that offers different coffee products and services. Our friendly workers are always ready to welcome customers and assist them with their orders. Customers can easily reach us and order their favourite coffee from our plantation.

Coffee Varieties

  • Arabica: A popular coffee variety known for its smooth taste and pleasant aroma.
  • Robusta: A coffee variety with a strong taste and rich flavour.
  • Batian: A Kenyan coffee variety known for good quality and resistance to some diseases.
  • Ruiru 11: A Kenyan coffee variety developed for good quality and disease resistance.

Coffee Production Process

  1. Planting: Coffee seedlings are planted in suitable soil where they can grow well.
  2. Caring for the Plants: The plants are watered, weeded, and protected to help them grow healthy.
  3. Harvesting: Ripe coffee cherries are picked from the plants.
  4. Drying: The harvested coffee beans are dried to reduce their moisture.
  5. Packaging: The dried coffee beans are packed ready for storage or sale.

Plantation Activities

  1. Pruning: Removing unwanted branches to help coffee plants grow well.
  2. Weeding: Removing weeds around the coffee plants.
  3. Fertilizing: Adding nutrients to the soil to support plant growth.
  4. Harvesting: Picking ripe coffee cherries from the plants.
  5. Drying: Drying harvested coffee beans before packaging.
